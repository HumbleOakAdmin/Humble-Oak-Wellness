About the clinic

Farmland and Mount Baker, looking east from Abbotsford.

Why we are here

We wanted a clinic in Abbotsford where you can get help for pain and movement without driving across the valley.

Humble Oak Wellness is part of the Humble Oak family, along with Humble Oak Solutions. This clinic is the health side of that work. We want you to have enough time in the appointment to say what is going on, and to leave knowing what we did and why.

You can book registered massage therapy or kinesiology. When the team is confirmed, we will add their names and a bit about how they treat.

Who we see

People who work on farms and in warehouses, parents, students, and weekend hikers on Sumas Mountain. If you live or work in Abbotsford, Mission, Chilliwack, or Langley, you are welcome. The oak in our name is the tree, and the colours are the fall colours of the valley.
