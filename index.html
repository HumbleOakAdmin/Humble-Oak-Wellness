Welcome

Humble Oak Wellness is a clinic in Abbotsford, and part of the Humble Oak family. We see people from Abbotsford, Mission, Chilliwack, Langley, and the rest of the Fraser Valley.

If your back is sore, a shoulder will not loosen up, or you want help getting back to work or sport, registered massage therapy and kinesiology are what we offer.

Massage for muscles and joints

Most people come in because something hurts, or because they have been tight for weeks and it is starting to get in the way.

Your massage therapist will ask what is bothering you, how you spend your day, and what you want to be able to do again. Treatment is hands-on work for muscles and joints. That might be your low back, a stiff neck, a headache, or a shoulder that flares up at work.
