Massage for back and neck pain, headaches, sports injuries, and muscles that stay tight from work.

What people come in for

If your neck, back, shoulders, or hips have been bothering you, massage therapy is a sensible place to start.

Your RMT will ask about the pain, what you do for work, and what you want to get back to. Treatment is hands-on work for muscles and joints. People also come in for headaches, sports injuries, pregnancy, and after an accident.
