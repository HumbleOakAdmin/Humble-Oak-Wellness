Registered massage therapy

Therapeutic massage for recovery, tension, and the physical effects of stress held in the body.

Care for what the body is holding

Stress and trauma do not live only in our thoughts. They can settle into the body as tension, pain, and fatigue.

Registered massage therapy addresses that physical side: tight shoulders from chronic worry, headaches from held grief, or a nervous system that never quite stands down.

At Humble Oak Wellness, massage therapy and counselling work as partners. Practitioners coordinate so you can heal from the inside out, and the outside in, without bouncing between disconnected providers.
